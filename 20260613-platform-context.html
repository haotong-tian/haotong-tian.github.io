Cost Diagnostic Platform

BCG runs "cost diagnostics" for large companies — where does your money go, how do you compare to peers, where's the savings opportunity? This platform automates that analysis: a consultant sends a one-email brief, a fleet of AI agents runs in parallel on a remote server, and ~45 minutes later an HTML dashboard, Excel workbook, and summary email come back. The question on the table is how to design the right UX for a system that does serious analytical work completely unattended.

Three options for composing the agent fleet

Fixed workflows, not agents

A library of locked pipelines. The orchestrator picks one and runs it end-to-end.
How it's organized
A run · Supply Chain BM

Limitation — every new ask requires a new workflow; PA knowledge duplicated inside each pipeline.

Persona-based

A typed roster of specialists. The lead briefs each persona and sequences them. (How Penny, our PMO agent, runs today.)
How it's organized
A run · Supply Chain BM

Limitation — the roster ossifies; new analytical shapes require a new agent type.

Ad-hoc teamed fleet

A fleet lead composes the right squad per request from a skill pool. Members can pull in their own helpers. This is what the platform uses.
How it's organized
A run · Supply Chain BM

Why this one — handles asks we haven't seen yet; PA nuance lives in skills, not pipelines; sub-agents can delegate like real associates.
